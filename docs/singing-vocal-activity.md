This is an unresolved permission question, not evidence that the authors prohibit research use.

The bounded model screen and baseline reproduction are complete. **The requested new-model accuracy evaluation is not complete.** No new detector was downloaded, installed, executed, or integrated. Its held-out metrics, boundary errors, additional-singer performance, and computational costs are unavailable. There is no measured improvement over the previous diagnostics and no failed-model accuracy claim. Existing mandatory review, timestamps, pronunciation overrides, corrections, TTML preparation, Swift rendering, and audiovisual export remain unchanged.

The product's mandatory automatic synchronization requirement remains incomplete. Activity detection cannot establish that the correct words or repeated occurrence were aligned. Deferred visual and product requirements remain deferred. The empirical visual reference remains iPhone 16, reported iOS 27.0.1, exact build **Unknown**; this study makes no native-fidelity claim.

## Prospective Evaluation Protocol

The [protocol](alignment-data/v4/protocol.json) fixes proposed metrics and decision rules before any new-model inference. **Concrete development, validation, and held-out manifests are not yet frozen**, because qualifying new recordings and annotation permissions are unavailable. The existing nine passages are audit material, not a newly independent final test. A later run must first fix audio/annotation hashes, singer groups, roles, and acoustic regions without consulting detector output.

The minimum proposed study uses separate development and validation singers and at least two further held-out singers, with four accompanied held-out excerpts of 20–60 seconds. Include sustained vowels, short phrases, pauses, reverberation, prominent instruments, at least twenty clear onset/offset pairs, and ten nonvocal gaps. Japanese mixtures require independently annotated lawful material; an isolated-Japanese auxiliary result must remain separately labeled. These sample counts support a bounded experiment, not population-level certification.

Reference annotation must include sung consonants and sustained vowels, tag backing vocals, humming, ad-libs and overlap separately, and distinguish instrumental sound from silence. Breaths, spoken interjections, disputed boundaries, and indistinguishable reverberation tails remain unknown. Store earliest/latest plausible boundaries; obtain independent review without model output. Unknown regions are excluded explicitly, with their duration and strict/permissive sensitivity reported.

Frame TP/FP/TN/FN use a common 10-ms center grid and exact comparisons against each model's native grid. Report precision, recall, F1, FPR and FNR; undefined denominators remain unavailable. Exact duration coverage and per-gap activation are separate. One-to-one interval pairing at IoU ≥0.5 prevents one merged prediction from counting as several correctly detected phrases. Report unmatched predictions/references alongside onset and offset median, p95, maximum, and errors above 500 ms. Do not independently register boundaries to hide a phase error.

Proposed engineering targets are precision, recall, F1, interval recall and vocal-duration coverage ≥0.90; nonvocal FPR and instrumental-gap activation ≤0.10; boundary median ≤100 ms, p95 ≤250 ms and at most 5% above 500 ms; warm runtime below ten times audio duration and peak RSS below 4 GiB. These are prospective screens, not established performance or a relaxation of alignment acceptance criteria. A favorable aggregate cannot override failure on new singers or instrumental gaps.

Run the documented recipe first. Any small threshold/smoothing comparison must use development and validation only, then remain fixed on held-out recordings. Report default and calibrated results separately. Fixed gain, resampling, and AAC sensitivity tests cannot become held-out parameter selection. Stratify by language, mixture/isolated audio, singer and acoustic condition. Report uncertainty by independent recording rather than treating correlated frames as independent samples.

Only after standalone VAD evaluation may detected support be compared with original lyric intervals. Unexplained vocals can be backing voices rather than omitted lead lyrics. Incorrect words, order, and repeated occurrences with identical vocal support remain acoustically ambiguous. Keep the frozen lexical comparator separate. No score becomes a correctness probability, and no result authorizes automatic acceptance, rejection, or timestamp repair.

## Next Mandatory Decision

**Do not proceed to automatic full-song segmentation.** There is no new evidence that vocal boundaries or instrumental discrimination improved, and no additional lyric-correspondence detections were measured. Mandatory review remains in place; automatic synchronization is not complete.

Resume this same bounded candidate, without restarting the survey: obtain checkpoint-scoped permission clarification; establish a minimal compatible CPU dependency plan and request approval for the concrete additional downloads; acquire and independently verify the additional-singer vocal labels; freeze the partitions; run the documented recipe and untouched comparators; then assess the prespecified criteria. If checkpoint permission cannot be established, record that terminal selection failure before requesting authorization for a different candidate. No user download approval substitutes for rights held by a model or recording owner.

A positive VAD result would justify only review-required unexplained-vocal flags. A subsequent full-song experiment would still require recordings longer than one minute, additional singers, repeated choruses, instrumental sections, segmentation without reference-derived clip boundaries, and explicit handling of residual lexical ambiguity. Those steps are not authorized as part of the present implementation scope.
