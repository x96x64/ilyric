# Bounded TIFA Passage-Candidate Search

## Outcome and Scope

October 9, 2026. This experiment adds an isolated, review-required passage search using the existing local TIFA model. It preserves the full-song CTC command, excerpt alignment, pronunciation overrides, correction provenance, TTML importer, renderer, project formats, and audiovisual backend. No model, dependency, dataset, or network acquisition was added. The governing scope remains one developer’s private, personal, noncommercial use.

The EN-F01 development experiment did not establish improved correspondence reliability. Provisional support increased, but newly qualified incorrect locations also increased. The new command is an explicit experiment, not the active replacement for CTC. Automatic acceptance, rejection, endpoint repair, and full-song completion claims remain disabled. Optional formal activity annotation is not a prerequisite for this personal engineering comparison.

## Reproduced Baseline and Development Decision

All three frozen full-song CTC artifacts were regenerated from the existing complete recordings and untimed inputs. Semantic JSON matched exactly after exclusion of runtime and memory measurements. The baseline remains 140 raw line proposals, 53 provisionally supported and 87 unresolved, with eleven corresponding-reference nonoverlap conflicts. Source audio, input text, model identities, and the private annotation manifest were retained. Historical evidence and reports were not overwritten.

EN-F01 contains substantial complete-sequence displacement: eight raw proposals have no overlap with their corresponding annotated line. Its two previously qualified lines do overlap their references. The initial TIFA experiment searched each two-line paragraph independently in automatic 30-second windows. It qualified only two lines; both were approximately 90 seconds from their references. Many reference-containing windows instead produced extended or weakly supported paths. This is direct evidence that a short supplied passage embedded in a window containing other vocals can receive plausible but incorrect forced timing.

One bounded development change added the preceding and following supplied paragraphs as pronunciation context. Only central-paragraph timing and span similarity are retained. It raised EN-F01 provisional support to twelve lines, six of which do not overlap their corresponding reference. The unchanged thresholds and contextual implementation were then frozen before EN-F02/03 evaluation. These two recordings are evaluation holdouts for this change, not previously unseen singers or independent population validation. No further parameter selection used their results.

## Candidate Architecture

`alignment/passage_worker.py` verifies the pinned TIFA source fingerprint and every existing model-asset hash before inference. It reuses the upstream spectrogram, pronunciation-path scoring, and alignment backend, with scope 1, frame-unit output, and skip penalty 0.5. CPU execution uses four Torch threads, fixed seed, deterministic operations, and offline dependency settings. Network access is denied. The local lightweight pronunciation dictionary is reused. No upstream source or model is modified.

The reused model is `TIFA-1.0-ST`, source revision `614a277d2580efe5e4b84faf4c34753dbc5a062c`, checkpoint SHA-256 `8d9bf896da1827f36bf135f80c7ab1fc4f3c1d48f328818b8402c9527c0f014b`. The archived MIT source-code and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 weight licenses remain distinct; this personal noncommercial experiment does not establish commercial integration or redistribution rights. No checkpoint is included in public history. Existing [alignment provenance](automatic-alignment.md) remains authoritative for licensing and recording provenance.

The complete recording is separately decoded to mono 48-kHz PCM16. Original audio is hashed before and after processing. Source-relative integer microseconds are derived from decoded sample counts. Thirty-second windows start every fifteen seconds, including the bounded final tail. Each request is below the established excerpt limit; audio longer than 600 seconds fails rather than being truncated silently. No vocal detector, reference interval, manual anchor, CTC-selected clip, or timestamp annotation chooses the windows.

Each supplied paragraph is a target occurrence, including repeated text. One preceding and one following paragraph provide context where available. Identical context/target combinations reuse inference without merging occurrence IDs. Whole-context token agreement and central-phone span similarity are recorded separately. Both are uncalibrated model diagnostics. Original text is preserved; the existing English normalization is used only for a checked occurrence-to-word mapping. Skipped context words remain diagnostic evidence and cannot erase valid central word support. Missing, overlapping, or text-inconsistent central units invalidate the candidate. Original paragraph breaks remain independent of model words.

Raw phone timing retains TIFA’s 10-ms grid. Central line bounds use their first and last supported word units. No interpolation fills skipped units. This preparation does not shape text, infer character timing, or modify Core Text. Existing English restrictions remain; Japanese full-song correspondence was not evaluated.

Resource bounds are 64 paragraphs, 64 audio windows per paragraph, 2,000 unique model evaluations, 4,000 expanded candidate records, 1,500 normalized context characters, and the existing pronunciation-lattice limits. These are explicit engineering bounds, not universal model capabilities. Temporary analysis audio and incidental pronunciation caches remain under the ignored output parent.

## Chronological Reconciliation and Uncertainty

The model-free `alignment/passage_search.py` keeps all candidate intervals and diagnostic rejection reasons. Eligibility requires finite agreement at least 0.65, central similarity at least 0.45, complete ordered line support, and central passage duration no greater than 25 seconds. Utility is the positive excess of the two-score arithmetic mean over 0.60. These fixed engineering values were selected before inference, not fitted correctness probabilities. Candidates whose beginning and ending differ by at most 0.5 seconds are treated as duplicate overlapping-window hypotheses; their votes are not accumulated.

A bounded dynamic program selects a maximum-utility chronological path with optional skipped paragraphs. Earlier endpoints with no smaller utility dominate later endpoints. A candidate cannot overlap a previous selected passage or reuse its physical interval for another repeated occurrence. Alternative paths are recomputed after excluding candidates substantially overlapping each selected location. A global utility gap below 0.05 marks that occurrence ambiguous. The margin tests distinct locations; it does not bound onset or offset error. Chronological reconciliation prevents a simple greedy late match from automatically displacing the next passage, but a globally consistent wrong path remains possible and was observed.

The record distinguishes provisional `supported`, `ambiguous`, `skipped`, and `unresolved` states. Support means eligibility and a sufficient ranking margin only. Skipped means eligible candidates existed but were not selected; unresolved means none passed the checks. Neither proves absent lyrics. Raw competing paths, rejected candidates, context omissions, and unassigned audio remain available for inspection. Unassigned intervals are not classified automatically as instrumental music or omitted vocals.

`estimated.json` uses the existing reviewed full-song artifact with a separate engine identity and 10-ms temporal grid. Supported selections remain pending review; ambiguous selections retain raw proposals but no effective estimate. Skipped and unresolved occurrences have no invented timestamp. Existing review commands preserve original estimates, accept explicit corrections with notes, and export only fully reviewed ordered results. Correction reuse does not import a model. [Experimental commands](tifa-passage-commands.md) document invocation and limits.

## Comparative Evidence

Comparisons use the same complete English recordings and manual word-derived line annotations as the archived full-song study. The annotations establish the stated reference convention, not independently reviewed vocal-activity truth or verified lexical identity. Timings enter only post-inference scoring. A reference-overlap failure is a strong temporal conflict; overlap alone cannot establish that the correct words or occurrence were matched.

The numerical record includes all proposal and qualified-subset errors, reference nonoverlap, narrow wrong-repeat overlap, gained and lost provisional support, passage states, candidate availability, model provenance, runtime, and memory. Candidate availability asks only after inference whether any generated candidate overlapped every line of its intended paragraph; it cannot select the operational path. This post-inference availability check is deliberately weaker than correct timing or lexical correspondence. It nevertheless identifies a generation/coverage limitation in addition to a ranking failure. EN-F01’s [development diagnostic](alignment-data/v9/development.json) shows overlapping scores for correct-region and conflicting candidates; increasing a single support threshold is not demonstrated to separate them. The [initial target-only failure](alignment-data/v9/initial-failure.json) remains archived separately.

These results do not justify replacing CTC or moving the principal development effort to correction efficiency. The search adds explicit uncertainty and inspectable alternatives, but worsens the measured correspondence outcome. EN-F03 loses support relative to its own CTC baseline and introduces fourteen qualified reference conflicts. There is no demonstrated reduction in manual correction burden: one hundred lines lack an estimate, and twenty-two additional qualified lines conflict with their references. Actual personal correction time remains unmeasured.

## Remaining Failure and Next Gate

The experiment separates candidate generation, score qualification, chronological selection, and user review, but does not establish reliable lexical correspondence. More provisional estimates cannot compensate for newly qualified wrong locations. A large ranking margin between TIFA candidates is not a correctness guarantee. Accompaniment, incomplete context, repeated passages, and permissive forced alignment remain plausible contributors; the current comparison does not isolate their causal contributions.

No personal listening review or correction-effort measurement was performed. Unresolved counts are a lower bound on explicit timing decisions, not measured user effort. The intended requirement of automatic timing with manageable correction remains incomplete. The retained single-user correction mechanism works technically, but correspondence reliability is insufficient to move the main engineering effort to correction-interface efficiency.

The smallest next corrective experiment is candidate-level lexical contradiction checking with the already provisioned English CTC model, applied to candidate regions rather than forcing the complete supplied sequence. Evaluate its standalone and additional rejection decisions on the archived candidates before changing selection. Preserve TIFA/CTC disagreement and abstention rather than averaging their scores or treating agreement as calibrated confidence. First determine whether the generated alternatives contain usable locations; lexical filtering cannot recover a passage absent from the candidate set. If candidate availability remains the limiting factor, test one bounded context/window-coverage correction on EN-F01 before another unchanged-recording comparison. No new model survey, acquisition, or visual work is justified by this failure.

Proceed with correspondence development; do not claim unattended synchronization or practical automatic completion. After correspondence becomes sufficiently reliable for personal use, measure single-user correction effort, validate complete-song export, and then resume native Music visual work. Existing physical-reference qualifications, model licensing distinctions, and the deferred inquiry-document cleanup remain unchanged.
