## Bounded Architecture and Timing

The isolated `scripts/align_full_song.py` command accepts local untimed UTF-8 lyrics, local audio, and the existing pinned English model. It retains the existing text intake: original text/hash, blank-line paragraph separation, explicit within-paragraph breaks, Unicode range mapping, at most 64 paragraphs, 499 UTF-16 units and four explicit lines per paragraph. This worker supports ASCII English letters and contractions, including curly apostrophes preserved in display. Japanese, accented alignment text, numbers, and unsupported symbols fail explicitly; the existing Japanese excerpt workflow is unchanged. No general multilingual capability is implied.

Complete audio is decoded through FFmpeg to mono 48-kHz PCM16, bounded to 256 MiB input and 600 seconds plus one rejection sample. Longer input is rejected rather than silently trimmed. SciPy `resample_poly(1, 3)` supplies 16-kHz analysis samples. Recorded decoded/analysis sample counts establish source-relative coordinates; duration is converted once to nearest integer microsecond. Estimated boundaries retain the model’s 20-ms grid. Video frames never define acoustic times.

Automatic fixed segmentation uses 30-second windows with four seconds of overlap. Each inference call remains below the established 60-second excerpt limit. Existing wav2vec2 processor normalization is applied per window. Central overlap ownership selects existing scores on the global 320-sample grid; it neither blends predictions nor duplicates frames. Ownership must be contiguous. The final unavailable receptive-field tail is explicitly recorded, rather than extrapolated. No reference-derived clip boundary, supplied timestamp, vocal-passage selection, or manual anchor enters inference.

A separate bounded monotonic CTC Viterbi path reconciles the complete supplied sequence over the stitched scores. It allows acoustic blank support before, between, and after lyric occurrences. Repeated characters require intervening blanks; repeated lines retain distinct source IDs and chronological positions. Bounds are 6,000 target labels, 30,000 frames, and 120 million trace bytes. This is an ordered forced-correspondence proposal, not a skip-capable lexical verifier. A wrong or omitted verse can still displace several subsequent lines. A failed complete path leaves every line unresolved; invalid window reconciliation fails the operation without a fabricated result.

TIFA is not applied automatically to these proposals: transferring incorrect coarse boundaries into another forced aligner would not establish correspondence. veracity is not used to cut the recording or select words. Its activity predictions remain provisional, and singing presence cannot identify the correct phrase or occurrence. Existing spectral, CTC, TIFA, lexical, pronunciation, and endpoint diagnostics remain intact.

## Support Flags and Review

The preparation artifact retains each raw proposal, original text/ranges, occurrence identity, model/checkpoint revision, dependency versions, source hash, window/sample coordinates, effective temporal resolution, diagnostic scores, and unresolved flags. Scores are not probabilities. A proposal becomes an unreviewed estimate only if mean forced character log support is at least −3.0, same-model greedy text similarity is at least 0.55, no character spans more than 1.5 seconds, and the line spans at most 20 seconds. These fixed engineering heuristics were selected before the complete-song comparison, without per-recording tuning. They are not independently validated rejection thresholds. Greedy and forced scores share a speech model and are not independent lexical evidence.

Repeated source text always receives an occurrence-review flag, including when support checks pass. Weak proposals retain their raw bounds but have no effective estimate. Unassigned audio is the complement of effective line intervals and is explicitly unknown; it is not automatically classified as an instrumental gap, silence, backing vocals, or missing lyrics. This representation preserves introductions, internal gaps, and trailing audio without inventing words or assigning all audio to text. It cannot diagnose every omitted or misordered passage.

The new experimental artifact is separate from the unchanged excerpt artifact and renderer project schema. All lines begin pending. Explicit batch decisions may accept existing supported estimates or supply corrected integer-microsecond bounds with notes. Original estimates and flags are integrity-checked and retained; corrections and history are separate. Corrections can be loaded, validated, and exported without importing a model. Overlap, reversed occurrence order, missing timing, and incomplete review prevent complete export. No acceptance is automatic.

Reviewed preparation produces the existing line-level TTML subset. Paragraph start/end use the first/last constituent line; explicit breaks and whole-paragraph shaping survive. Internal line gaps retain paragraph focus under the existing policy. No word/character highlighting is manufactured. The TTML sidecar must remain with its provenance artifact. A version-3 project or existing direct input invocation can consume it without a schema change. [Commands and limits](full-song-commands.md) document the preparation workflow.

## Runtime, Reproducibility, and Export

The measured environment remains Apple M4, 16 GiB, arm64 macOS 27.0.1, isolated Python 3.12.14, four CPU threads, fixed seed, and deterministic Torch operations. The existing `facebook/wav2vec2-base-960h` revision `22aad52d435eb6dbaf354bdad9b0da84ce7d6156` and all local asset hashes are checked before loading. The existing Apache-2.0 model provenance remains recorded in the original alignment study. No inference download is permitted. TIFA code/weight licensing and checkpoint-specific veracity uncertainty remain separate and unchanged.

Final complete-song preparation took 9.73, 11.70, and 12.21 seconds internally, including asset checks, imports, preprocessing, inference, and reconciliation, but excluding interpreter startup/exit. Preprocessing took 0.243–0.269 seconds, model initialization 0.030–0.034 seconds, window inference 4.795–6.615 seconds, and reconciliation 1.584–3.299 seconds. Peak process RSS was 1.840–2.016 decimal GB. These observations overlap other verification work and are not an isolated performance comparison. Repeated EN-F01 runs produced identical semantic artifacts after excluding explicitly recorded measurements.

The public synthetic check begins with original untimed lyrics and deterministic synthetic acoustic scores, not pretrained inference. It exercises a 75.003-second source, introductions and gaps, repeated lines, late-song events, explicit two-line paragraphs, review of eight occurrences, a 123-microsecond correction, artifact reload, and TTML export. The unchanged AVFoundation path emitted 4,501 H.264/AAC frames at 1080×1920 and 60 fps, with uniform `contain`, limited-range Rec.709, 75.016667-second output, and expected subframe audio padding. All presentation timestamps passed. Supplied-audio correlation was 0.999969 with zero measured lag; six original markers had zero measured timing error. AAC priming was 2,112 samples and decoded trailing padding 544 samples. These are container/audio checks, not singing accuracy.

Export took 141.40 seconds, or 31.83 frames/s. Raster and backpressure counters overlap and must not be added. A decoded late-song still showed the expected final paragraph and established controls, without a new layer or layout change. No visual-fidelity claim follows from this inspection. Real-song video export remains pending actual user review; only the synthetic correction/export path is demonstrated.
