# Implementation Readiness and Open-Source Release Plan

## Decision and Evidence Status

October 9, 2026. iLyric should retain its deterministic Swift rendering architecture and offline preparation boundary, but replace the unsuccessful text-conditioned passage-search strategy with **audio-derived anchors, occurrence-preserving sequence matching, and bounded boundary refinement**. The first proposed acoustic index is multilingual Whisper `small` through `whisper.cpp`; the existing English CTC model refines only passages with sufficient correspondence evidence. This is an implementation hypothesis, not a demonstrated singing-alignment result. No alternative model was provisioned or executed during this study.

The limited fallback is legitimately supplied synchronized LRC or TTML, validated against the actual recording. It does not satisfy the mandatory automatic-alignment requirement for untimed input. Music cache access, provider subscriptions, TIFA's restricted weights, and unqualified veracity predictions must not become prerequisites for the public product.

**No-go for a usable public release today. Go for one bounded correspondence implementation.** Working rendering experiments are not a supported product, and the repository has no selected software license. Public source availability alone does not constitute an open-source licensing grant. This report updates the intended distribution scope without changing historical results or personal evaluation permissions.

Evidence categories are explicit below: repository measurements are historical observations; external capabilities and licenses are documented claims; the architecture, thresholds, and milestones are proposed requirements. All external sources were accessed October 9, 2026. Licensing analysis identifies engineering obligations and uncertainties, not legal advice.

| Evidence | Demonstrated Result | Consequence |

| [English excerpt alignment](automatic-alignment.md) | TIFA onset/offset p95 approximately 339/866 ms; CTC approximately 2454/240 ms | Onsets and endpoints fail differently; successful decoding is not correct correspondence |
| [Complete-song CTC](review-first-full-song.md) | 140 proposals, 53 provisionally supported, 87 unresolved, eleven raw reference-nonoverlap conflicts | A forced complete sequence can drift; zero conflicts in the qualified subset does not establish lexical correctness or adequate coverage |
| [TIFA passage search](tifa-passage-search.md) | Forty qualified lines, one hundred unresolved, twenty-two qualified reference conflicts; six qualified wrong-repeat overlaps in EN-F03 | More plausible forced paths did not improve correspondence; do not promote this search |
| [Veracity](local-veracity-inference.md) | Reproducible local CPU inference; exploratory activity outside word/phone unions remains substantial | No independent activity accuracy established; singing presence cannot identify words or chorus occurrences |

Japanese results benefited from bounded, known excerpts, pronunciation mapping, and a small corpus with phone-derived reference conventions. Those conditions remove much of the passage-selection problem. They do not contradict poor complete-song English results. An English lyric occurrence can be forced onto a different vocal passage, particularly when similar choruses recur. A monotonic path can remain globally wrong while locally plausible. Only 33 of seventy TIFA target paragraphs had any candidate overlapping all corresponding reference lines; ranking alone cannot recover absent candidates.

Sustained vowels, accompaniment, pronunciation variation, and extra or omitted sung material can distort acoustic scores. Their individual causal contributions have not all been isolated. Forced terminal paths can absorb unrelated vocals; the previous silence-aware repair failed held-out validation and remains disabled. Spectral activity falsely marked instrumental gaps, while CTC activity covered only approximately 13–20% of annotated vocal duration in reserved English passages. Neither supplies a reliable hard segmentation mask. Boundary accuracy, vocal coverage, lexical identity, and occurrence identity require separate evidence.

Historical operational observations on Apple M4/16 GiB include approximately 10–12 seconds and 1.84–2.02 decimal GB peak process RSS for the three-song CTC runs, versus 257–1466 seconds and 1.27–1.33 GB for contextual TIFA search. Concurrent workloads and different algorithms prevent controlled speed comparisons. Personal correction time remains unmeasured. None of these results completes mandatory synchronization.

## Proposed Preparation Architecture

1. **Immutable intake:** hash original audio and complete text; preserve paragraph/occurrence identities, including identical choruses. Decode analysis audio separately. Retain sample-rate conversion and exact source-offset provenance.
2. **Audio-only index:** evaluate bounded overlapping windows with the pinned recognition model, without supplying lyric prompts or carrying previous lyric hypotheses into the next window. Retain raw hypotheses, timestamps, omissions, and overlapping alternatives. Do not discard regions solely because a vocal detector marks them inactive. Recognition is internal evidence, not a requirement that users supply a transcription.
3. **Correspondence lattice:** map normalized recognition and supplied text through reversible source ranges. Use monotonic, gap-aware sequence matching with explicit audio insertions, missing supplied passages, alternative paths, and occurrence identities. Unique neighboring phrases constrain repeated choruses; ambiguous identical occurrences remain unresolved. Overlap reconciliation must not merge distinct repetitions. Freeze scoring on development material and retain rejected alternatives.
4. **Bounded refinement:** apply the existing English CTC model only inside supported anchor regions with bounded context. Preserve disagreement between recognition and forced alignment, unsupported endpoints, unmatched words, and competing locations. Do not average scores into a purported correctness probability or interpolate missing lines. Refinement cannot create lexical support absent from the correspondence stage.
5. **Validated preparation:** output original text/ranges, raw estimates, model identities, integer-grid boundaries, uncertainty, unresolved regions, and review provenance. Derived TTML retains a lossless preparation sidecar. Corrections preserve the original estimate and can be reused without inference. The Swift renderer consumes prepared inputs offline, unchanged.

Japanese needs a separate text-to-reading mapping: written forms, morphological units, kana readings, morae, phonemes, graphemes, and shaped clusters are not interchangeable. Preserve explicit pronunciation overrides; do not choose readings by reference-error minimization. Whisper's Japanese support does not qualify Japanese full-song alignment. English contractions and repeated words likewise require occurrence-specific mapping. The initial automatic release scope should be **English line-level timing** if it passes the gates below; Japanese supplied-timing rendering can remain supported within its existing limits. Japanese automatic timing and automatically generated fine-grained highlighting require separate qualification. Explicitly supplied fine-grained LRC/TTML remains a different capability.

The first experiment must compare candidate availability, occurrence selection, and boundary quality separately on EN-F01, then unchanged EN-F02/03. These are familiar regression recordings, not new independent holdouts. Stop if audio-derived anchors do not reduce wrong-occurrence proposals while preserving coverage. Do not compensate by making all lines unresolved or lowering support thresholds. A failed anchor experiment should identify whether the next single correction concerns recognition coverage or sequence reconciliation; it does not authorize another broad survey or production integration.

## Evaluation and Release Acceptance

The following are **prospective engineering targets**, not achieved results. Initial support excludes overlapping lead singers, translated lyrics, mismatched editions, and languages not qualified. Unsupported or uncertain input must produce explicit diagnostics; exclusion cannot silently yield a plausible video.

A single maintainer can annotate lawful recordings while blinded to predictions, retain uncertain boundary ranges, and conduct a separate review pass. This is practical engineering evidence, not independent multi-annotator research. Keep the existing three recordings as regressions. Prepare a six-recording English qualification set: two familiar recordings may serve as development data, but reserve four newly supplied or appropriately licensed evaluation recordings before parameter selection. The third historical recording remains an additional regression. Target at least three recording sources/performers where available and at least 300 lyric occurrences across the qualification set. Include complete 2–6-minute accompanied songs, introductions, breaks, repeated choruses, sustained vowels, and trailing audio. New acquisition requires permission and resource approval. Japanese later requires its own complete-song set; isolated PJS excerpts cannot substitute.

Preserve source hashes, exact editions, lawful local-processing evidence, reference conventions, ambiguous/censored boundaries, and split membership. Private source material remains private; public fixtures use original text, synthetic audio, and deterministic artificial model outputs. Those fixtures test software, not singing accuracy. Controlled omitted, extra, reordered, and repeated-text cases supplement natural failures; report them separately. Never select thresholds on the locked set.

| Gate | Proposed Acceptance Condition |
| --- | --- |
| Complete-song correspondence | At least 95% of all supplied occurrences automatically placed at the correct occurrence on **each** supported evaluation song; at most 5% unresolved. Zero unflagged wrong-chorus or multi-line displacement events in the locked set. Report all counts and uncertainty; zero observations is not a population guarantee. |
| Boundary accuracy | Onset and offset separately: median absolute error ≤100 ms, p95 ≤250 ms, and errors >500 ms ≤5%, using the declared acoustic convention. Report maxima, uncertainty-range sensitivity, and both all-proposal and supported-subset denominators. Missing proposals cannot disappear from coverage scoring. No unflagged error exceeding two seconds. |
| Failure detection | At least 90% recall across at least forty realistic mismatch controls, with per-class counts; ≤5% false flags among at least one hundred matched occurrences. Report false acceptance/rejection, ambiguity, and instrumental versus omitted-vocal failures separately. Small samples do not certify a universal rate. |
| Correction burden | No more than 5% of occurrences require manual boundary entry; active editing ≤one recording duration and total review ≤two recording durations per song. Measure wall-clock interaction, playback, edits, and reruns separately. A full manual retiming fails even if the final video is accurate. |
| Operations | Complete preparation ≤2× recording duration and peak process-tree memory ≤4 GiB on the qualified Apple Silicon/16-GiB configuration, after reporting initialization separately. Serial inference; cancellation/resume without source alteration. These are targets, not Whisper performance claims. |
| Determinism and export | Reordered/repeated rational evaluations produce identical snapshots and raw frames in the pinned environment; reviewed preparation round-trips without models. Validate full duration, dimensions, cadence, color, supplied audio, presentation endpoints, and no lost final paragraphs. Proposed 1080×1920/60-fps export target: ≥20 rendered fps and no unexplained >10% peak-memory growth between comparable two- and ten-minute scenes. |

Word, phoneme, or mora metrics may be reported where references support them, but cannot replace line-occurrence qualification. Fine-grained automatic output must be withheld when boundaries cannot map safely to whole-paragraph shaping. Acoustic errors and audiovisual-container synchronization remain separate measurements. Current CTC/TIFA systems fail the proposed correspondence gate; correction time remains unmeasured. No new accuracy was measured here.

The [MIT license](https://opensource.org/license/mit) minimizes administration but lacks an express patent grant. [Apache-2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) adds an express contributor patent grant and notice/change obligations. [MPL-2.0](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/FAQ/) requires source availability for covered modified files while allowing larger differently licensed works. [GPLv3](https://opensource.org/license/gpl-3-0) imposes broader corresponding-source obligations for covered distributed combinations; it allows commercial use. Process separation is not an automatic exemption from licensing obligations.

**Recommend Apache-2.0 for the maintainer's consideration**, given the proposed permissive dependency set and explicit patent provisions; it is not selected by this report. MPL or GPL is appropriate only if reciprocal licensing is an intentional project policy. A future contribution guide should specify the chosen inbound/outbound terms and a lightweight provenance attestation, with no assumption that historical contributors granted additional rights. Keep license/NOTICE inventories and an SBOM synchronized with exact distributed binaries and model artifacts. Review material unresolved legal questions before distribution rather than inventing permission.

## Product Integration and Visual Qualification

The present `ilyric` executable still exposes the synthetic spike; real project rendering and preparation use separate probes/scripts. The smallest integration is a supported facade over existing intake, prepare/review, validate, and render operations—not a new backend or plugin framework. Stabilize only after the timing artifact works. Preserve version-1 through version-3 experimental project interpretation or provide explicit, tested migration; strict unknown-field rejection must remain.

Current input limits include ten minutes, 64 paragraphs/events, 64 KiB timing files, bounded text/spans, and mono/stereo audio restrictions. Qualify a coherent initial subset rather than silently truncating songs, grouping unrelated lines, or promising universal TTML. TTML's secure namespace/interval subset, enhanced-LRC semantics, explicit breaks, exact rationals, source Unicode, glyph-cluster refusal, and whole-paragraph Core Text shaping remain invariants. Review-only provenance must survive derived TTML without exposing private paths in shareable projects.

Initial user-facing support should be macOS 27.x on Apple Silicon with 16 GiB, limited to versions actually tested before release. The existing macOS 14 package declaration is not a tested support promise. No Windows, Linux, Intel Mac, or iOS distribution is proposed. Pin Swift/Xcode SDK and the minimal isolated worker runtime; use explicit model manifests, hashes, notices, and resource estimates. Missing optional models must fail actionably before export. No global Python modification, hidden network access, or account credentials are acceptable.

The visual target remains native Music on **iPhone 16, reported iOS 27.0.1, exact build Unknown**, under the preserved reference settings. macOS Music and web Music are not substitutes. Existing reports support bounded candidates, not native fidelity:

| Area | Remaining Work and Qualification |
| --- | --- |
| Typography and wrapping | Preserve Latin/Japanese candidates, supplied breaks, and S04/S05 uncertainty. Validate automatic wrapping independently; a font match conditional on observed breaks is insufficient. |
| Progressive/inactive appearance | Preserve cluster-aware operations and script-specific fitted parameters. Validate multiline transitions and cross-script inactive appearance with held-out states; do not transfer Japanese fits to Latin automatically. |

Visual qualification should freeze reference states and comparison regions before fitting, retain source uncertainty, and report geometry, color/appearance residuals, motion timing, and full-sequence inspection separately. Proposed static geometry target: p95 ≤5 native pixels for measurable text/component anchors; investigate every new wrapping discrepancy. Appearance and material thresholds must be fixed from reference repeatability before fitting, not chosen afterward to declare fidelity. No single pixel score or matching crop proves full native behavior.

Apple's [Xcode/SDK agreement, section 2.10](https://www.apple.com/legal/sla/docs/xcode.pdf), does not establish unrestricted standalone-video export rights for system-provided images. SF Symbols capability documentation is not that permission. Retain original vectors as the default; [Lucide's ISC/MIT notices](https://lucide.dev/license) provide an alternative subject to attribution and separate visual calibration. Do not bundle extracted icons or proprietary fonts. Any redistributed alternative font needs its own license and new calibration. Rights to an application symbol, recording, lyric, artwork, and resulting video remain separate questions.
